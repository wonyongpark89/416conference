About the Conference

Critical Reflection, Cross-Sector Dialogue & Creative Collaboration

This conference gathers scholars, educators, archivists, disaster-affected families, practitioners, policymakers and community organisations from around the world to explore new directions in disaster education. It is designed as a space for critical reflection, cross-sector dialogue and creative collaboration, positioned at the intersection of memory, resilience, social justice, preparedness and the pursuit of peaceful, safe communities.

The event takes place at the 4.16 Institute, a nationally significant site for remembrance, learning and public engagement following the Sewol Ferry disaster.

The Venue

4.16 Institute for Life & Safety Education

The Institute serves as a major archive and educational hub dedicated to democratic citizenship, safety education and the preservation of memory related to the Sewol Ferry disaster. It houses an extensive collection of documents, artefacts and physical objects gathered since 16 April 2014.

The Danwon High School 4.16 Memory Classrooms — intact classrooms preserved as they were on the day of the students' final attendance — are among its most significant holdings. The Institute provides programmes for students, teachers, communities and professionals, supporting ongoing public reflection and action for a safer society.
